Unnamed · How it works
Same coins. Higher ceilings.

Launching and trading works exactly like pump.fun — free creation, bonding curve, 85 SOL to graduate, no rules against sniping. The difference: every coin here owns a vault that buys its dips and can never dump on you, flippers who leave early pay the chart they left, and size exits through a desk instead of through the candles.

The vault

Stake SOL at launch and it becomes the coin's market maker. It buys dips, sells only into strength above what holders paid on average, and can never withdraw. You keep up to 10% of everything it makes.

Flippers pay the chart

Sell profit in the first 30 minutes and up to 8% of the profit goes to that coin's treasury — not to us. The fee melts to zero by minute 30. Losses always exit free.

The desk

Buyers escrow SOL in resting bids. Hit one and your sell fills instantly at −3–5% instead of −20% AMM slippage — and it prints to the tape, never the chart.

Copies pay the original

Launch a copy of a running coin and half its fees stream to the original's vault. Copy holders get a one-tap swap into the real one via the desk. The goal isn't fewer copies — it's making the real one run harder.

Burn to redeem

Every vault backs a book value. Burn your tokens and take your pro-rata share of the treasury, any time. Every redemption leaves more backing per remaining token — the book can't be drained.
