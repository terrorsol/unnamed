BANKER6
Security

Every signature is checked before it's signed.

  • Simulated first. The exact transaction is simulated and every transfer it would make, inside every program it calls, is walked before the keeper signs.
  • Hard limits. Allowlisted programs only. The vault is the only signer. SOL out is bounded by the trade. A buy has to show the coin arriving.
  • No withdraw path. Keeper caps per transaction and per hour. The dev is paid from realized profit only.
  • Attacks refused on mainnet. A hidden 0.5 SOL drain, a token delegate, a foreign-token transfer and an oversized sell, each slipped into a real trade, were all refused.
3

independent reviews of the money path. Every finding fixed, each with a test.

48

checks against mainnet on every deploy: real bundles, real buys and sells, real attacks.

212

automated tests: 100 on the server, 112 end-to-end.

BANKER8
The Vault

A market maker the holders own.

  • Buys in block zero, before any sniper.
  • Taxes the snipers: half of every early buy sold back into it.
  • Sells into pumps when price stretches over VWAP.
  • Buys dips in tranches, only under VWAP, from its own chest.
  • Stands on the desk as buyer of last resort and no-fee seller at spot.
  • Never withdraws. The dev is paid a cut of realized profit only.
