BANKER4
The Vault

A market maker the holders own.

  • Sells into pumps when price stretches over VWAP.
  • Buys dips in tranches, only under VWAP, from its own chest.
  • Stands on the desk as buyer of last resort and no-fee seller at spot.
  • Never withdraws. The dev is paid a cut of realized profit only.
  • Funded by the 2% tax, not by the dev.

BANKER7
The OTC Desk

Sell a block. No candle.

  • One button. Sell into the best bid, or buy the best offer.
  • Bids rest 1–15% under spot. Offers either side. Block requests ping every holder.
  • Non-custodial. One on-chain transaction, signed by both sides.
  • The vault is always quoting, from block zero.

BANKER11
Also shipping

The same machinery, more ways in.

Import any coin

A vault for a coin launched elsewhere: pay the fee, or let the community crowd-fund a takeover.

Burn-to-redeem

Any holder can burn tokens for their pro-rata share of the chest. The floor is real, not implied.

Block requests

Name a size and a max loss; resting bids sweep it and every holder gets pinged for two minutes.

Vault deposits

Holders add SOL or tokens to the chest. Bigger bid, bigger dip buys, same rules.

Dev cut, renounceable

The creator earns a % of realized vault profit and can renounce it on chain in one click.

Terminal built in

In-app wallet, quick buy, charts from the pool, runner signals, phone-first. Phantom and Solflare too.
